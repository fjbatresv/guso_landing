The app groups everyday care tasks into a single patient experience.

## Care and discovery

Patients can search doctors, clinics, and specialties, explore clinic information, and manage medical appointments.

## Personal health

The app supports medical prescriptions, a patient health record, insurance information, and reminders for visits and doses.

## Helpful content

Health tips and promotional content provide a lightweight content area alongside transactional care flows.
